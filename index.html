✦ The soft reset issue · a note from Sheema

if I’m being honest,
this week has been a lot.

And instead of dressing that up and making it sound prettier than it is, I just wanted to talk to you like a real person.

I’ve had one of those weeks where everything feels a little heavier. My energy has been low, my mind has been full, and I’ve just been doing my best.

Come sit with me ↓

still here. still hopeful. still choosing softness. ♡

Okay, so what now?

We find our way back, little by little.

But even in the middle of that, I still believe in softness. I still believe in starting over. I still believe a hard week does not mean a bad life.

So if you landed here and you’re having a rough time too, hi. Me too.

Let’s not pressure ourselves to have everything figured out right now. Let’s just breathe, be gentle with ourselves, and take the next small step.

That’s enough for today. Truly.

And if nobody has told you lately, I’m proud of you for making it this far. ♡

xoxo, Sheema
🎧

Currently getting me through

The smallest things are bringing me back.

A good song. Actually doing my skincare. Laughing at something stupid. Sitting somewhere quiet for five minutes. Letting a bad day be a bad day without deciding everything is bad.
