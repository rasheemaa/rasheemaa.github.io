This week’s note

A lot of us need to touch some grass, respectfully.

I don’t get out much. Like honestly, I really don’t. And I feel like we can all agree that technology has taken over completely because why are we always on these damn phones? 😭

Recently I’ve been spending more time with my family than I usually do and honestly, it was very much needed. I didn’t realize how much I needed to actually be around people.

I can love being alone and still need connection. I can enjoy being around my family and still get anxious after a while. Maybe I just need a little more balance. And maybe some grass. 🌱
