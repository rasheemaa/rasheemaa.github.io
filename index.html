✦ The unexpected forgiveness issue · a note from Sheema

I didn’t know forgiveness
could arrive this quietly.

For years, I thought holding on to the hurt was how I protected the younger version of me. Then life gave me new context, and a door I was sure was closed opened just a little.

Nothing that happened disappeared. I just learned that understanding someone and abandoning myself are not the same thing.

Come sit with me ↓

the past stayed true. the future still changed. ♡

Okay, so what changed?

I made room for a new chapter without rewriting the old one.

Recently, I let someone from my past back into my life. If you had told a younger version of me this would happen, she probably would not have believed you.

I carried that hurt for a long time. Part of me thought the grudge was proof that I remembered what happened, proof that I was still loyal to the child who had to live through it.

Then I learned there were pieces of the story I never had before. That new understanding did not erase the impact or turn pain into something acceptable. It simply made room for complexity.

I realized I could forgive without pretending. I could open a door slowly. I could keep my boundaries and still let my heart put down something heavy.

Maybe forgiveness is not saying it was okay. Maybe it is deciding the pain does not get to write every chapter that comes next. ♡

xoxo, Sheema
🎧

Currently getting me through

Letting two truths exist at once.

The younger me deserved protection. The person I am now deserves peace. I do not have to choose one truth in order to honor the other.
