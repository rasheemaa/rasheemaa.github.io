This week’s note

I really thought forgiveness would feel different.

I thought once I forgave the people who hurt me, stopped carrying the anger, and let karma handle what wasn’t mine, I would finally feel free. Like something inside of me would click and I could breathe.

But it didn’t happen like that. You can forgive somebody and still hurt. You can tell yourself you’re safe now, and somehow your body still reacts like you aren’t.

There are parts of my story I’m not ready to tell. What I can say is that this week has been hard, and I’m showing up as I am.
