Lip Gloss, Little Joys, and Laughing Anyway

August 16, 20264 min read
joy can be
tiny and shiny

Not every meaningful moment arrives with a life lesson and soft piano music. Sometimes the thing that saves the mood is finding the lip gloss you thought was gone forever. Sometimes it is a ridiculous voice note, a song that makes the kitchen feel like a music video, or an outfit that understands the assignment even if the day absolutely does not.

Small joy is not shallow joy. It does not need to solve your life to belong in it.

Healing is serious work, but you are still allowed to be unserious on purpose.

Beauty can be play, not pressure

There is a difference between getting ready because you think you must hide and getting ready because color, texture, and shine feel fun. One is a demand. The other can be a tiny art project you get to wear on your face.

Maybe today it is a full look. Maybe it is moisturizer and a bonnet. Maybe the beauty ritual is leaving yourself completely alone. The point is choice. You are not more worthy when polished, and you are not less yourself when you enjoy the sparkle.

Funny stories are survival snacks

Some experiences are not funny while they are happening. Let us be extremely clear about that. But later, once everybody is okay and the group chat has assembled, the story can grow a perfect pause, one unbelievable detail, and a punchline you did not order.

Laughter does not erase what was hard. Sometimes it simply reminds the nervous system that hard was not the only thing in the room.

Collect the tiny shiny things

A good snack. Fresh sheets. The first cool breeze after a hot week. Your child saying something unintentionally hilarious. A package arriving on the day you forgot it existed. The exact right meme at the exact wrong moment.

None of these moments need to become a productivity hack or a gratitude challenge. They can just be nice. Let the little joy be little. Let it sit beside the complicated stuff. Let it wear lip gloss.

Today’s tiny assignment ♡Notice one thing that made the day two percent cuter. No deeper meaning required.
