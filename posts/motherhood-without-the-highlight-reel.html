Motherhood and family life

Motherhood Without the Highlight Reel

August 20, 20264 min read
love lives
in the real part

Motherhood contains a kind of love so huge it can make an ordinary Tuesday feel sacred. It also contains snack crumbs in impossible places, a drink you reheated twice and still forgot, and someone needing you at the exact second your brain finally loaded one complete thought.

Both versions are true. The tenderness does not cancel the exhaustion, and the exhaustion does not make the tenderness less real.

You can adore your child and still need a moment where nobody touches you or asks for the blue cup.

The whole picture belongs in the frame

Online, motherhood is often edited into two dramatic lanes. It is either glowing and effortless, or it is complete chaos served as a joke. Most days live somewhere in the middle. You can laugh at the mess and still feel overstimulated by it. You can crave a break and miss your child as soon as the room gets quiet.

There is nothing wrong with you for holding opposite feelings at the same time. That is not ingratitude. That is being a full human while caring for another full human.

Tiny care still counts

Self care during family life does not always resemble a silent spa with cucumber water. Sometimes it is eating before the afternoon disappears. Sometimes it is wearing the headphones, asking for help, sitting down while the toys stay exactly where they are, or choosing the easiest dinner with confidence.

The goal is not to become endlessly patient and beautifully regulated every second. The goal can be repair. A pause. A softer next choice. A reminder that your needs did not stop existing when you became somebody’s mother.

Ordinary love is still magic

Family memories are not only made during matching outfits and perfectly planned outings. They happen during the sleepy cuddle, the silly voice, the same story for the fourteenth time, the kitchen dance break, and the moment everybody laughs so hard the hard day loosens its grip.

The highlight reel may look shinier. The real version has fingerprints all over it. I think that might be what makes it ours.

For the mom reading this ♡Needing rest does not mean you love your family less. You are part of the family worth caring for too.
