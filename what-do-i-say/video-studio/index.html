Sparkle Creator Studio / Safety hold

Imagination deserves
safe foundations.

AI video creation is temporarily unavailable. We will not activate a video generator until enforceable safety checks are in place and independently tested.

Generation disabled

Video creation is paused

To protect children and prevent abusive content, this preview has no video generation, upload, prompt export, embedded third-party generator, or external generation link.

Our planned safeguards prohibit depictions of minors in generated videos, child sexual abuse material, and sexual violence, including fictional, animated, transformed, or age-manipulated representations. Suspicious or uncertain requests must be denied, not sent to a generator.

No videos can be generated from this page. Existing Sparkle writing and voice features are separate.

Return to Sparkle chat

What must happen before video creation returns

We must enforce server-side screening of every prompt and reference upload, repeat checks after edits or regeneration, review generated frames and audio before any download, block uncertain results, and test bypass attempts. Third-party generation must never be reachable through an uncontrolled embed or direct handoff.

Until those protections work end to end, the safest configuration is to keep video generation off.
