How to use for $0: Prepare and copy your prompt, paste it into the embedded generator, then select Generate Video. Start with a 1-second clip and turn off High Resolution to use less free GPU time. No photo is required. If the generator cannot sign you in here, open its official page instead.

Free means no surprise bills. 💗

Sparkle Video Studio does not call paid AI APIs, collect payment information, or run a GPU server. The external Hugging Face ZeroGPU service supplies shared GPU time with daily usage limits and queues. Free users may run out of their quota; videos can fail, take time, or be unavailable. Hugging Face can automatically use prepaid credits on some paid accounts after their free GPU allowance is spent. For guaranteed $0 on that service, stay on an anonymous or free account without paid credit usage, and do not purchase upgrades or credits.

When you use the embedded generator, your text, uploaded images, and generated content are processed by its third-party operator, not locally in Sparkle. Only upload photos you have permission to use. This prototype doesn't offer a Sparkle-hosted video library or guarantee Google Flow results.
