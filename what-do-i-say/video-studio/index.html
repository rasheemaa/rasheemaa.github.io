02 / Video generation

Generation is safety locked

Not launched

Video generation and prompt preparation are disabled until Sparkle controls the full process and can enforce moderation before generating and before delivering a result.

Nonnegotiable rules: No depictions of minors in any context, including realistic, animated, and fictional characters. No sexual violence, coercion, exploitation, or content sexualizing a person who is or appears underage. Ambiguous ages are not approved. There are no exceptions.

A third party generator was removed from this preview because its content moderation cannot be enforced by Sparkle. No video generation provider is accessible from this page.

A safe launch matters more than a fast launch. 💗

Sparkle will not open video generation until server enforced input checks, upload analysis, output review, and abuse controls can block prohibited videos. Text warnings and client side filters do not count as enforcement. While the safety gate is incomplete, this page cannot generate videos or prepare prompts.

See the Creator Studio safety release requirements in the project repository. The preview remains unlaunched; the existing Sparkle voice and chat features are separate.
