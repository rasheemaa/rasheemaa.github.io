What to expect

The initial model download is around 146 MB and may take several minutes. The model may be cached by your browser. Older phones may run slowly or run out of memory. Voice similarity and iPhone Safari compatibility are not yet verified.

This beta tests voice cloning only. It does not listen through your microphone, run an ongoing conversation, or replace the existing Sparkle chat. No new account, payment, or recording is required.
