Privacy & compatibility

Sparkle’s owner chose to make the approved voice reference available to every visitor. Your browser downloads Sparkle_reference.wav directly from The Sheema Edit website. The reference recording is publicly accessible, so everyone can hear Sparkle without a OneDrive link or uploading a file. No personal conversations are included in the sample. The free local voice model can be slow or fail on some phones.

Speech recognition may use a service provided by your browser. Recognized text and recent conversation turns go to Sparkle's Cloudflare AI. This is a turn-by-turn beta, not continuous real-time voice. Server AI usage may cost the site operator.
