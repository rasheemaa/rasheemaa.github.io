What can this mode do?

Uses actual footage licensed CC0. It can resize, add one of three fixed titles, and export a five-second video locally where your browser supports canvas recording. Otherwise it downloads a pre-rendered version with your selected format and title. It does not invent scenes with generative AI and never processes people's likenesses.

Wondering whether your desktop could eventually run local AI video? Check local AI readiness. This checks reported GPU requirements and exports a project plan only. No AI model runs, no uploads occur, and generation remains safety-locked.
