Privacy

What Do I Say? is designed to keep message content limited to the services needed to generate your draft. Processing differs by device so Sparkle can stay reliable.

How message generation works

  • On iPhone and iPad: the message text you submit is sent securely to the What Do I Say? Cloudflare Worker and processed with Cloudflare Workers AI so Sparkle can generate a response without loading a large AI model inside Safari.
  • On supported desktop browsers: Sparkle may continue to process message generation on-device in the browser.
  • Generated drafts are returned to your browser and may be stored locally as part of the product experience.

The What Do I Say? application does not intentionally write your message text or generated draft into its server logs. Operational logging is designed to use content-free error and request information.

Creator Studio

Building a creative brief, editing a draft and managing saved projects use your browser's storage. Your Creator Brain contains the name, niche, writing preferences and topics you choose to save on that device.

When Creator AI is available and you choose Generate with AI, Sparkle sends the reviewed task, idea, audience, tone and saved Creator Brain to its Cloudflare backend for generation. Saved projects and backup files are not uploaded automatically. Sparkle does not publish posts, send replies, contact brands or read social accounts from Creator Studio.

Project backups and moving between addresses

Export project backup downloads a readable JSON file containing project names, ideas, preferences and saved drafts. Anyone with that file can read its contents, so keep it somewhere private. Choosing a backup for restoration reads it in your browser; it is not sent to the AI service.

Restoring a valid backup adds projects and keeps existing projects and the current draft. Projects with conflicting IDs are kept as separate copies. Project backups do not contain Creator Brain settings.

Saved work belongs to this browser and the exact website address. It does not transfer automatically between devices, between a preview and the public site, or between addresses with and without www. Use project export and restore to move saved projects.

Video plans

The video planning workspace stores saved plans in this browser under a separate video project store. Tab recovery keeps the current plan in this tab. Plans contain your concept, scene direction, timing, visual and sound preferences. No plan is submitted to a video service, and no video or audio is generated. Downloading a backup exports the current video plan only. Restoring a backup opens an unsaved plan and does not replace saved projects. Video plans do not sync between devices or website addresses.

Browser storage

  • Your browser may store Creator Brain settings, projects, saved drafts, unsaved-draft recovery data, product preferences and legacy purchase information from earlier versions. Sparkle no longer requires trial or purchase verification for access.
  • The app may cache interface files and local model files needed for normal operation.
  • Clearing site data can remove local projects, drafts, profiles, preferences and legacy purchase-recovery information. Export important projects first. A downloaded backup is a separate file that you manage.

Payments

Sparkle is free and does not require checkout. Stripe handled earlier purchases and may retain the associated transaction records.

Service providers and technical data

When Sparkle uses Cloudflare Workers AI, Cloudflare processes the generation request as the infrastructure and AI provider. Requests to the site and its providers can also include normal technical information such as an IP address, browser information, and request timestamps.

Security

Do not enter passwords, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or other credentials into the message box. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can delete saved projects, saved drafts or Creator Brain settings using the relevant Creator Studio controls, or clear this site's browser storage through your browser. Deleting browser data does not delete a backup file you already downloaded and does not affect free access.
