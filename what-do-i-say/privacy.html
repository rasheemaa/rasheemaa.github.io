Privacy

What Do I Say? is designed to keep the message you are working on private. Sparkle runs on your device after its model files load, so the text you type for message generation is not intentionally sent to The Sheema Edit payment server.

What stays on your device

  • The message text you type into the tool and Sparkle's generated drafts are processed in the browser for the on-device experience.
  • Your browser stores limited information needed for the three-day trial, purchase verification, and return access.
  • The app may cache files needed to run Sparkle and support offline-style app behavior.

Payments

Checkout is handled by Stripe. The payment Worker receives only the information needed to create a Stripe Checkout session and verify whether the correct purchase was completed. Card details are entered with Stripe, not into What Do I Say?.

Model and app downloads

Your browser may contact content delivery networks or model-hosting services to download the files Sparkle needs. Those services may receive normal technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, and request timestamps. Your drafted message is not included in those model-file download requests by What Do I Say?.

Security

Do not enter passwords, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or other credentials into the message box. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can clear this site's browser storage or cache through your browser controls. Doing so can remove trial and purchase-recovery information stored on that device, so keep site data enabled if you have purchased access.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the product changes. The date at the top shows the current version.

This page describes the current product behavior and is not legal advice.
